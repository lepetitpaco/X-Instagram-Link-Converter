Share links that embed.

One click copies an X, Instagram or TikTok post as a link to an existing embed service (vxTwitter, FxEmbed, fxTikTok…), so Discord, Telegram, WhatsApp & co show the real video or picture. The embeds are theirs.

Add to Chrome Add to Firefox
Free, open source, no tracking.
How it works

Buttons right where you share

Each site gets its own buttons, on every post. Pick the format, the link is in your clipboard.

X / Twitter

Chips in the action bar: vx (fixvx), upx (fixupx), vxt (vxtwitter), or x for the clean original link.

TikTok

Chips under Share copy a tnktok, tiktxk or kktiktok link. On the For You feed, the popup grabs the video on screen.

Instagram

A link button next to Share on every post and reel, and kkinstagram in the Share menu, whatever your interface language.

Your services

Add, hide or reorder services per site from the popup, no extension update needed. Extra ones wait in a compact ⋯ menu.

Private by design

No tracking, no analytics, no account. Links are converted in your browser. Privacy policy

Light

Plain JavaScript, three permissions, no remote code.

Open source

Ideas or a broken embed service? Open an issue.
